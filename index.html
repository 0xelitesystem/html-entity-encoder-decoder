HTML Entity Encoder and Decoder

Encode and decode in your browser

Encode text to HTML entities or decode entities back to plain text. Handles named entities and numeric entities (decimal and hex). Updates live as you type. Nothing leaves this page.

Decodes named entities (&amp;), decimal numeric (&#169;), and hex numeric (&#xA9;).
All encoding and decoding happens locally. Your text is never sent to any server.
